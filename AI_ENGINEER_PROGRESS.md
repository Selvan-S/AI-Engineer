## 8. Learning Rules

1. Prefer implementation over passive consumption.
2. Understand concepts before memorizing APIs or frameworks.
3. Ask "why?" and "when would I use this?" for important tools.
4. Build small exercises before larger projects.
5. Do not skip foundations merely because the goal is GenAI.
6. Prefer understanding the underlying problem before learning a framework.
7. Use documentation and AI assistance, but verify and understand generated code.
8. Revisit weak areas instead of simply moving forward.
9. Explain important concepts in your own words.
10. Build end-to-end projects before considering a topic complete.
